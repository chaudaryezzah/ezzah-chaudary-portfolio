Sample Editorial Pitch

By Ezzah Chaudary · 1 min read

A short demonstration of how to frame a relevant story idea around a publication’s readers rather than a promotional agenda.

Portfolio sample: This original demonstration is not represented as previously published client work.

Proposed headline

“The Small AI Habits That Help Students Think for Themselves.”

Why this story, why now?

Students and educators are navigating a growing set of AI tools, but advice often swings between uncritical enthusiasm and blanket rejection. A practical, balanced article could help readers distinguish uses that support learning from uses that bypass it.

Intended audience and reader benefit

Parents, educators, students, and readers interested in learning and digital culture. The piece would offer concrete ways to use AI for practice and feedback while keeping independent reasoning central.

Proposed outline

  1. 1
  2. Why the way a student uses AI matters
  3. 2
  4. Examples of AI as a quiz partner or explainer
  5. 3
  6. How to verify claims
  7. 4
  8. Questions schools and families can use to set expectations
  9. 5
  10. A practical checklist for keeping the learner involved.

Research and editorial fit

The final article would use relevant education research, current institutional guidance, and interviews with qualified educators where appropriate. The pitch would be tailored to the target publication’s actual sections, audience, contributor guidelines, and recent coverage.

Professional note

This fictional example demonstrates pitch structure. It does not imply a relationship with a publication, a commissioned assignment, or guaranteed acceptance. Any commercial relationship or sponsored content should be disclosed and handled according to publisher policies.
